# Research Question

**Main question:**
Do popular Android apps collect more data than their declared privacy labels disclose?

**Sub-questions**
1. Which app categories request the most dangerous permissions?
2. How many third-party trackers does each category embed?
3. How often do requested permissions / trackers imply data types missing from the Data safety section?
4. How do findings compare with DPDP Act / GDPR transparency expectations?

**Hypotheses**
- H1: Apps in <category> request more dangerous permissions than <category>.
- H2: A majority of apps have at least one undisclosed data category.

**Scope:** Android only, <N> apps, <categories>, versions tested on <date>.
